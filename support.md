## FAQ

**Is my text stored anywhere?**
No. Checks are processed transiently and your history lives only on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

**Why did the app say "Depends on context"?**
Some Korean sentences are correct in casual speech but not in formal writing (or vice versa). Instead of forcing a wrong binary answer, the app tells you honestly that it depends.

**What does the "NIKL sourced" label mean?**
The grammar point behind that correction links to a reference from the National Institute of Korean Language (국립국어원) — so you can verify the rule yourself.
